I built `battery.mdl` (in the server's work folder), and the build says OK with every check passed.

**How it's made**
- **Body:** a solid shaped from the outlines in the front, left, back, right and top pictures, cut off at the body's top edge. That gives it the squared octagonal cross-section, the bevel round the bottom and the bevelled top rim. Every view's outline matches the original's to 0.999 or better.
- **Top fittings, built by hand:** the shaping method fills in any hollow that no picture sees into, so a first try turned the tilted tab into a solid wedge. I built these instead:
  - an eight-sided knob
  - the block the tab sits on, and a bar joining it to the knob
  - the yellow-striped tab, tilted about 51 degrees
- **Colours and markings:** all ten of your pictures are projected onto the shape as its texture, at 512 x 512. That covers the green glowing windows, the white side panels, the red lights, the yellow-and-grey hazard band, the dark bottom and the grey top.
- **Size:** 10.3 long, 10.3 wide and 18.4 high, standing on the floor.

At the four close angles (front-left, front-right and the two back corners), my renders line up closely with your pictures: outline, knob, tab, windows and stripes.

**Where it differs**
- **Bottom:** the original has no bottom face, so looking from below you see through it to the tab inside. Mine has a closed bottom painted with your below picture. From straight below it looks nearly the same, but from below at an angle you won't see into it.
- **Triangles:** it has 156. The build warns that Half-Life's props of this size usually have under 88. That's a warning, not a fault, and it's well within the engine's limits.
- **Small top details:** the knob's notches are only in the painted picture, not built. The top rim is slightly softer than the original's crisp bevel.

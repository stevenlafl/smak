**Overall**

The map is an indoor industrial complex, roughly octagonal, with chamfered corners. It spans about x −1480 to 944 (west to east) and y −1000 to 1040 (south to north), so about 2420 × 2040 units. North is +y. It has three main walking levels: the ground floor at z 0, a mezzanine at z 160 and catwalks at z 320. A tall central atrium has small ledges at z 512 and a roof at about z 600–640. The wing rooms have ceilings at about z 448–470, crossed by downstand beams about 24 deep every 256 units or so. The outer walls of the two wings lean inward above z 320, up to the roof at 640.

**Look and light**

- Walls are grey stained concrete with a recessed band of dark computer racks and grilles at eye height (about 40–110), behind rounded frames. A yellow-green stripe runs along the base.
- Floors are dark brown industrial tile or grating. The spine corridor has bluish grey metal floor plates and orange hazard trim on the ceiling beams.
- Light is dim and cold, from fluorescent ceiling strips about every 256. The NE annex is the exception, lit warm orange by its lamps.
- The atrium is the brightest space, lit from the roof light well.

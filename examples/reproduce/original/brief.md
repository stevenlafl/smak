# Flooded Works: the brief

A small mod for Half-Life, played in the game's own folder: its files replace or sit beside the game's, so it
runs with no new code. Make every asset below, to a quality that holds up beside Half-Life's own.

## The place

An old municipal pumping station, half flooded since the pumps failed. Concrete, rusted steel, yellow hazard
paint, green-grey water, sodium lamps that buzz. Industrial and worn, in Half-Life's own palette: no neon, no
sci-fi.

## Maps (three, played in order)

1. **fw_yard**: the station's yard at dusk, open to the sky. A high wall round it, a gatehouse with a door
   that opens, stacked drums and a broken truck-sized pipe section. The player starts by the gate and leaves
   through the pump house's double doors. About 1500 by 1500 units.
2. **fw_pumphall**: the flooded hall. A long room, its floor under water deep enough to swim, three big pumps
   along it, catwalks above the water on both sides joined by a bridge, ladders between water and catwalk. The
   wrench lies on a catwalk; three crawlers in the water. Leaves by a stair up to a corridor. About 2500 long.
3. **fw_control**: the control room above the hall: consoles under a row of windows looking down on the hall,
   a lift down to a valve room where the main valve wheel is, which ends the mod. Two crawlers.

Each map leads to the next where the player can walk to it, and arrives where the last one left.

## Props

- a valve wheel, 32 across, red paint worn to steel at the rim
- a 55-gallon drum, rusted, with a hazard stripe
- a control console, waist high, with dials, two screens and a row of switches
- a pipe junction: two pipes meeting at a flanged elbow, bolted
- a caged wall lamp with a sodium-orange glass

## Weapon

A **pipe wrench** in place of the crowbar: its first-person, in-hand and world models, keeping the crowbar's
hands and animations, about 18 long, red-painted handle worn to bare iron, a heavy toothed jaw.

## Enemy

A **sludge crawler** in place of the headcrab's model, so the game's own headcrab plays it: a low, wet,
six-legged thing the size of a headcrab, grey-green with a pale belly and a ring of teeth, that keeps the
headcrab's animations and everything the game's code asks of the model.

## Textures (one .wad, fw.wad, at least twelve)

A concrete wall, a wet concrete floor, a steel grate with holes, a catwalk floor, a pipe-covered wall, a
hazard-striped trim, a rusted steel plate, a control panel with dials, a sodium lamp's face (lit), the water,
a sign reading "PUMP 3", and a door.

## Sprites

A steam puff that rises and thins (animated), a sodium lamp's glow, and a sludge spit that splashes
(animated).

## Sounds

The wrench striking metal, the wrench's swing, the crawler's attack and its pain, a pump's droning loop, and
water dripping in a loop.

## To show it

A turntable video of the wrench and of the crawler, a flight through the pump hall, and a sheet of pictures of
every prop.

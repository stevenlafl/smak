I built `work:filecabinet.mdl` and the build says OK: every check passed. It's 12 triangles on one 512×256 texture, which is about what Half-Life's own filing cabinet uses (14 triangles, everything else painted on).

**Shape:** one box, 22.6 wide, 28.8 deep and 62.4 high, standing at floor level, with sharp corners and a flat top.

**What's painted on, checked in the six side views and a large three-quarter view:**
- **Paint:** dark charcoal with cloudy mottling and grime, heavier toward the bottom. A little wear shows on the edges.
- **Base band:** a darker strip 3.4 high runs round all four sides, with a bright line along its top edge.
- **Drawers:** three fronts, each 19.5 wide and 17.3 high, centred with about 1.5 of body showing at each side.
  - Spacing is 1.7 above the top drawer, 1.2 between drawers and 3 above the base band.
  - Each is a raised bevelled panel, lit from the top left, with a dark recessed outline and a shadow line underneath.
- **Handles:** black cup pulls, 6×3, centred and a little above each drawer's middle. Each has a light rim on top and a darker curved scoop below.
- **Left side:** fine vertical streaks in alternating lighter and darker greys across the front 45% of the depth; the rest is plain mottling.
- **Right side and back:** plain mottled charcoal.
- **Top:** mottled, with a thin light line along the front edge.

**Gaps and things I'd flag:**
- **Underside strip missing:** the light strip along the front edge of the underside isn't there. Painted markings can only go on the front, back, left, right and top faces, so the underside is plain mottled grey.
- **Drawers slightly taller:** I made them 17.3 high instead of about 17. With the 1.7 / 1.2 / 3 spacing and the 3.4 base band, that's what fills the full 62.4 height.
- **Colour tint:** in the kit's renders the grey shows a very faint warm or purple tint from the paint's colour variation. It's not a colour I chose, and the game's lighting will look different from these renders anyway.

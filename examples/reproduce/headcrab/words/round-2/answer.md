`headcrab.mdl` is built and the build says OK: no holes, nothing loose, and the pictures don't see through it anywhere.

**Size.** It came out at exactly the asked size: 25.9 long, 19.2 wide and 13.2 high, standing on its claw tips at z 0. The body covers the rear 15.5 units (60% of the length) and the forelegs reach forward for the rest.

**What it has:**
- **Body:** a sack-like dome, highest at the front. The front face is flat and slopes back and down, and the back curves down to the floor. The skin is tan-ochre with dark brown streaks along it and ribs across it. Two raised ridges run along the top edges and meet in a V at the rear, which shows from the back. The underside is flat and stained dark red.
- **Pale membrane:** a cream bulge about two thirds of the body's width. It covers the front face and runs over the top to mid-body, with white blotches, thin red veins and red staining along its lower edge.
- **Mouth:** a red ring under the rear-centre, with 10 off-white peg teeth and a pale inside. The teeth show under the rear edge from behind.
- **Forelegs:** each has a thin tan upper segment from the upper front corner, then a sharp bend into a thicker blood-red forearm with darker blotches. Each ends in an ivory claw hooked down, with a black band, touching the floor. The two legs run parallel, as wide apart as the body.
- **Hind legs:** short thick stumps at the rear corners, angled out a little. They are tan at the top and red at the foot, with two small black hooked claws each.

The texture is 128×128 pixels, so the brown areas look blocky the way the original's do. There are no glows, lettering or lights. The model has 1,096 triangles, inside the engine's limits but on the heavy side for a small creature.

I've built `headcrab.mdl` in the work folder (`work:headcrab.mdl`) and the build says OK with every check passed. It's 776 triangles on one 512×256 texture, which is within GoldSrc's limits.

**Size:** 25.9 long, 19.5 wide, 13.1 high, against your 25.9 × 19.2 × 13.2. The extra 0.3 of width comes from the rear claws flaring out a little.

**Shape:**
- **Body:** two parts. An upper shell is wide and flat across the top, and a lower belly narrows toward the floor. Together they give the original's broad-shouldered front view and its slanted, lozenge-like side outline.
- **Front legs:** each has a thin brown thigh rising to a high knee, then a thick red forearm that splays outward going down, ending in a dark claw at the very front.
- **Rear legs:** these come down from the shell's back corners to bulbous red feet, with claws pointing back and out.
- **Fangs:** two small dark ones under the front of the belly, like the original's.

**Colours and markings:** these come straight from your six straight-on pictures in `sides.png`, projected onto the shape. So the cream dome with its red speckles, the brown ribbed shell, the red legs and the toothed mouth underneath are the original's own pixels. I had to give the outline box for each view by hand, because the tool's automatic detection took in the view labels and threw the alignment off. With the boxes fixed, nothing sees through the model, and the view from below shows the mouth and teeth where the original has them.

**Differences you can still see:**
- From the top, the rear edge of the shell is straight. The original's comes to slightly pointed corners out toward the rear legs.
- The front face of the dome is a bit rounder and less flat-slanted than the original's.
- The four corner views weren't used to paint it, because their exact camera angles aren't known. I checked them by eye: the front-left view matches well. From the back-right, the original's body sits a little lower over its rear legs.

`work:medkit.mdl` is built. The build passes every check, and the engine check says it fits GoldSrc: 268 triangles, one 512×512 texture, a single bone and one body. The spec is saved next to it as `work:medkit.spec.json`.

**Size and outline**
- It measures 34.6 long (front to back), 26.1 wide and 6.8 high. The width is 0.3 short of the 26.4 asked for, about 1%.
- The box is 23×23. The walls are vertical for the first 3.4 units, then 45° bevels rise to a 16.2-unit top panel.
- The upright corners are cut short, so from above it's an octagon with long sides, as described. The kit's round shapes can only make an even octagon, which would have made the corner cuts as long as the sides. So I built the shell from three flat sheets instead: the walls with their bevels, the top and the bottom.
- The strap loops out about 12 units in front of the box to a crooked point slightly left of centre. Where it leaves the side connectors it stands on edge, half the box's height. It leans over as it runs forward and lies nearly flat at the front.

**Markings**, all painted into the texture:
- On top, a large red cross near the back edge, with "FIRST AID" in red capitals in front of it. The words read upright from the back.
- The device panel is a dark grey plate running from the top down the front bevel. It has:
  - a light grey strip with a black dial at its right end;
  - a green screen in a black bezel on the left;
  - a red button above a green one, with a column of four white dots beside them.
  
  From the front it reads as a dark window in the white slope.
- Each side bevel carries a line of small red "FIRST AID KIT" lettering and a red arrow pointing to the back. There are small red crosses on the four corner bevels.
- Each side wall has a black inset toward the front holding a grey and black grommet, where the strap attaches.
- The shell is off-white and mottled, with grime painted in. The strap is khaki-brown woven cloth.

**Where it may differ from the original:**
- **Side lettering:** at this texture size it shows as a thin red line of marks rather than readable words.
- **Pictograms:** the strip shows plain white marks, because the kit's print only does capitals and digits.
- **Thin sheets:** the shell and strap are flat sheets drawn on both sides. The strap has no thickness, so where it stands on edge it shows as a line from above.
- **Strap path:** the loop follows my reading of "rough teardrop" and may not match the original's exact path.

I checked it from all six sides and up close on the top and the panel.

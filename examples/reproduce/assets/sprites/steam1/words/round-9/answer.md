**How it plays:**

- **Frames 1-3:** it bursts into a lumpy, opaque white cloud that grows quickly in the lower middle.
- **Frames 4-7:** it swells to nearly the full width. It's bright white in the middle with grey shading on the edges and underside, and its top rises.
- **Frames 8-11:** it dims to light grey, gaps open up and it stands taller.
- **Frames 12-15:** it thins into faint grey vapour and fades to nothing.

I tuned it over four builds: one cloud layer of joined, lumpy puffs, shaded from grey to white, with the edges faded so no light touches the border.

A single still frame, 32 pixels wide by 64 tall, drawn additively over black. It is one straight segment of a laser beam that the engine tiles end to end and stretches along the beam's path, so the texture runs vertically and must tile seamlessly from top to bottom.

A bright core band runs the full height, down the middle, about 8 to 10 pixels wide. It is close to pure white at the centre and stays just as bright from top to bottom. On each side the brightness falls off quickly into a soft halo of pale grey, very slightly cool and bluish, that fades to black about 2 to 4 pixels from the left and right edges. The core is a little off-centre, leaning a pixel or two to the left, and the right halo is slightly wider and brighter than the left.

The halo is not smooth. It is streaked with faint vertical striations and blotchy, mottled darker patches, like smeared noise or energy flowing along the beam, so the edges look ragged and alive rather than clean. The streaks run parallel to the beam. Brighter and dimmer stretches alternate along the outer edges every 10 to 20 pixels, but the white core stays steady.

There is no colour of its own beyond white and grey. The game tints it, so a white-to-grey greyscale texture is right. Because there is only one frame, nothing animates within the sprite. Any flicker or scrolling comes from the engine moving the texture along the beam.

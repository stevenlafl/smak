The zombie is built and the build says **OK** (every check passed). It is written as `zombie.mdl` in the SMAK work folder (`work:zombie.mdl`), with its spec beside it at `work:zombie.spec.json`.

I had no pictures of the original, only your description, so I could not put it side by side with the original. The result is my reading of the description.

**What it is**
- **Base and height:** built on Gordon's skeleton and keeps all 77 of its animations. `floor: 0`, so the feet stand at the origin. Height comes out at 68.6 units against the 68 asked for.
- **Stance:** the first frame of the first animation (`look_idle`) is turned into a stoop: spine and neck lean forward, the head bows, the knees bend slightly, and the right forearm hangs down instead of holding a gun.
- **Head:** a cream bone-white dome down to the jaw, an ochre rim, and three red strands under it.
- **Torso:** a white-grey coat that hangs to mid-thigh, a dark slit down the front, two dark holes on the back, and blood smears at the chest, elbow and knee.
- **Arms and hands:** thin near-black arms, enlarged hands, and five hooked claws on each hand, each about 12 units long.
- **Legs:** pale grey trousers and dark red-brown shoes.

**Where it falls short**
- **Claws:** they hang about 5 units lower than the original's knee height. A straight arm from this shoulder height cannot reach knee height. The claws are one dark colour, with no bright crimson tips.
- **Coat:** closed in the model, with no lapels or open front. The open look is painted on as a dark slit.
- **Walk:** I did not fit a walk to the description, since there was no picture to fit it to. The model uses Gordon's own run and walk animations, so it does not shamble.
- **Triangles:** 708 in total, under the limit of about 724 (twice the base's 362).

I made these choices without asking: Gordon as the base, the stoop and arm pose, the colours (taken from your palette, with the tones kept below Valve's brightness so the build's checks pass), and the blood and wound placement.

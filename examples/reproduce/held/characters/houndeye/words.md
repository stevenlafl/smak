A squat, headless alien creature on three legs, about the size of a large dog. It is 49 units long, 34 wide and 45 high. Its body is a single rounded lump, like a fat bean or a tick, and there is no neck or separate head. The body takes up the top two-thirds of the height and the legs the lower third. Seen from above, the body is a teardrop: broad and rounded at the front, narrowing to a blunt point at the rear.

Face: the whole front of the body is one big oval eye-cluster. It sits in a recessed socket edged with a thin pinkish-red rim, and the socket is filled with about a dozen glossy, near-black dark-maroon spheres packed in a grid, like a compound eye or a cluster of eggs. Each sphere has a small white highlight. There is no mouth on the front. On the underside, just behind the eye, is an oval mouth or vent of dark red flesh with pale tooth-like ridges inside.

Skin: smooth and leathery, a mottled olive-to-mustard yellow-green with darker brown wrinkle lines and fine, vein-like cyan streaks. The belly and lower flanks fade to pale cream, also lined with faint blue-green veins. On each flank sits a small dark puckered spot like a navel or pore.

Back markings: a ridge of six or seven low, triangular, backward-leaning plates or spines runs from just behind the eye to the rump. Between and across them run bold stripes that wrap down the upper sides like chevrons pointing forward: bright turquoise-cyan bands alternating with dark chocolate brown, golden yellow and cream. From above, this reads as a striped, banded back like a beetle's or a bee's.

Legs: three thin, double-jointed legs, two at the front shoulders and one at the centre rear. Each starts in a bulging, wrinkled thigh muscle of the same yellow-green skin, tapers into a bony shin that bends backward like a hind leg, and ends in a small splayed foot with two or three pale cream toes. The legs are pale and knobbly toward the feet. The front legs splay slightly outward, and the rear leg sits right under the tail point.

Pose: standing, the body hunches forward with the eye tilted slightly down, and the back arches up toward the shoulders.

Movement: it runs in a bounding, galloping gait. The body tips forward with the eye low toward the ground. The front pair pull in under the chest while the rear leg kicks far back and straight out behind. Then all legs gather under the belly before the next push, so the body rocks and lunges forward and up in hops instead of walking smoothly. The spines stay rigid and the body doesn't bend; all the motion is in the leg joints and the pitch of the whole lump.

The model is built and the build says OK: `houndeye.mdl` in the work folder (240,928 bytes), with the spec saved beside it as `houndeye.spec.json`.

**Base:** I used `headcrab.mdl`, not Gordon. Gordon's skeleton is humanoid with two legs. The headcrab has two front limbs on its neck and a pelvis-hung pair, and it is close to the houndeye's size. The base's own body is 478 triangles, and the model is 700, under the twice-base limit. The spec sets `"floor": 0`.

**What is in it:**
- **Body:** one smooth teardrop turned along x. Broad front, blunt rear point, about 48 long, 36 wide, 45 high.
- **Eye cluster:** a flat front plate with a pinkish-red rim, a dark socket, 12 dark maroon round "eyes" with small white highlights. These are painted, not built, to save triangles.
- **Spines:** six dark brown triangular plates on the back, leaning back, from behind the eye to the rump.
- **Stripes:** eight chevron bands across the upper body, cycling cyan, brown, gold and cream.
- **Belly:** a pale cream band along the underside.
- **Mouth:** a dark red oval on the underside behind the eye.
- **Flank pores:** two small dark spots.
- **Legs:** two splayed front legs (olive thigh, pale shin and paw) and one centre rear leg under the tail point, bent backward at the knee.

**Checks:** the build passes every check. The walk, run, crouch and jump frames show no parts coming apart. The first-frame dimensions are 47.5 long, 36 wide and 44.8 high against 49, 34 and 45 asked for.

**Not done or approximate:**
- The teeth ridges inside the mouth are missing.
- The toes are single pale paws, not two or three toes each.
- The pose is level. The original's forward tilt of the eye and arch of the back are not applied.
- The eye plate is a flat square with its corners standing off the round body.
- Colours are chosen by eye from the description. I had no picture of the original to compare against.
- The animations are the headcrab's, not the houndeye's own bounding gallop, since the user asked to keep the base skeleton's animations.

I have not run it in the game, so the walk is untested beyond the kit's checks.

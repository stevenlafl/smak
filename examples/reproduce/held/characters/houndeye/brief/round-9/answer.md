   - Two painted fold lines go round the body as segments.

   - The thighs and shins taper.
   - Each hind leg has a knee knob.
   - Each foot has a paw and two claws.
   - The front leg is now a bent, tapering tube hung from the neck, so it has a bend. It still moves with the head, because the skeleton has no bone of its own for it.
3. **Paint and face (flat yellow, no detail, a black eye with no lids or red iris):**
   - The body has more form shading, which the kit's light setting controls.
   - The stripes taper to points, and there are two thin dark veins on the back.
   - The eye has a red iris, a black pupil and a thin upper lid.
   - The eye's pale yellow is now a muted yellow, because the picture check flagged the near-white.

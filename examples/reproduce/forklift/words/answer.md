`forklift.mdl` is built. The last build passed every check, and the separate engine check says it's fit for GoldSrc: 1,978 triangles, two 512×512 textures, one bone.

**Size:** 186.6 long × 61 wide × 113.4 high. The extra unit of width is the wheel hubs, which stick out 0.5 past each tyre.

**What's in it, checked against the six views and close-ups:**
- **Body:** a red lower chassis band. Each side has a "HAIHAN" plate with white stripes above and below it, between the wheels. Above that is grimy off-white bodywork.
- **Cowl:** a rounded hump in front of the seat, with a dark steering column and a ring-shaped steering wheel.
- **Counterweight:** tall, with a rounded top-rear edge and grey vent grilles on top. Each side has the "2.2 / LITER" badge, a small black logo, three grey dashes and a yellow tag at the top rear corner.
- **Back face:** a dark recessed slot, a yellow number plate, round dark lamps at the lower corners, and the red skirts showing at the bottom.
- **Wheels:** four black tyres with grey hubs. The front pair is radius 12 and sits just behind the mast; the rear pair is radius 11 and sits under the counterweight.
- **Mast:** full-height dark steel uprights, a black top bar, and an orange-brown chain column inside each upright.
- **Backrest:** full width, knee height, with a rounded opening in the middle and ribs at the sides.
- **Forks:** two thin tines about 70 long, 20 apart, each with a narrower tip.
- **Overhead guard:** a see-through dark mesh roof with black edges, a little lower than the mast top. The two rear posts are vertical; the two front posts slope down to the cowl.
- **Driver:** yellow hard hat, pale skin, green coverall, tan boots on a red floor step, both hands on the wheel, sitting on a black seat.
- **Underside:** grimy dark grey with red edges.

**Where it falls short:**
- **"LITER" is cramped.** The text tool spreads letters across the whole face, and the 512-pixel texture limit leaves the badge few pixels. The full word only fit once the badge plate spanned the whole counterweight side. Even so, the L and R sit right at the plate's edges. The "2.2" above it reads cleanly.
- **The logo is a plain black dot**, not a real emblem.
- **The fork tips taper in steps:** each tip is a narrower, thinner block, not a smooth point.
- **The counterweight's back is flat.** Only its top-rear edge is rounded, not the whole back panel.

**Things that cost builds:** the kit's flat paint patches erase any text printed on the same face of a box. The text also gets clipped when its face has too few texture pixels. That's why the HAIHAN and 2.2 LITER text sit on thin plates of their own. There's also a leftover test model, `printtest.mdl`, in the work folder that I couldn't delete with these tools. You can remove it.

The pictures are only the kit's flat stand-in for the engine's lighting, and nothing was loaded in the game.

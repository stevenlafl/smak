I built `forklift.mdl` (in the work folder, with its spec `forklift.spec.json` next to it). The final build says OK and passes every check. It's 186.6 long, 60 wide and 113.4 high, exactly the size you gave, and uses 2,128 triangles in 2 textures. I looked at it from the front, back, both sides, above and below, and close up on the lettering and underside.

**What's in it:**
- **Body:** a red lower band with "HAIHAN" in white and thin white stripes above and below, on both sides. The upper body is off-white with grime shading. The front cowl is rounded, and red skirts sit at the back corners. The underside is dark grey with red edges.
- **Counterweight:** a white block with a slightly curved back. Each side has a "2.2 LITER" badge, a small black logo, three grey dashes and a yellow tag near the top rear corner. Grey vent grilles run along the top edges. The back has a dark slot, a yellow plate low in the centre and dark round lamps at the lower corners.
- **Wheels:** four black tyres with grey hubs, the front pair larger and just behind the mast.
- **Mast:** two dark steel uprights at full height with a black bar across the top. Orange-brown chain runs down the inner channels. The backrest plate spans the full width with a rounded opening in the middle and ribs at the sides. Two thin forks with narrower tips sit 20 apart at floor level.
- **Overhead guard:** a see-through dark grey mesh roof with black edges. The two back posts are upright, and the two front posts slope down to the cowl.
- **Driver:** seated on a black seat, facing forward with both hands on the wheel. Yellow hard hat, pale skin, green coverall with darker creases, tan boots on a red floor step. A dark steering column comes up behind the cowl.

**Where it differs from the original:**
- **Lettering size:** "HAIHAN" and "2.2 LITER" come out smaller than the panels they sit on, about a third of the panel height. Making the setting or the panel bigger didn't change the letter size. It reads clearly up close but is small in the full side views.
- **Logo:** a plain black blob, not a real logo design.
- **Fork tips:** each tip is a thinner, narrower piece at the end of the fork, so it steps down rather than tapering smoothly.
- **Driver:** built from simple rounded shapes, so up close they look blocky.
- **Lift chain:** links painted on a flat strip, not separate links.
- **Lighting:** the pictures use the kit's stand-in light. I haven't tested the model in the game, so the engine's own light will look somewhat different.

**How it compares:** I drew it from the same front-left and back-right angles as your pictures. The outline, proportions and colours line up closely, and from the six straight-on sides it matches well. The main differences you'd see side by side:
- The body is built from square boxes, so the original's rounded rear body and sloped dash come out squarer at the corners.

I didn't use your four three-quarter pictures in the texture, only to compare against. They have some perspective, and projection works best from straight-on views.

# 35 / 03 "Now make it spring" (a change request)

## The prompt (20 words)

> And finally spring: blossom on all the trees, the grass fresh bright green, a clear bright sun. Nothing else changes.

**Score: 10 of 10 (100%), judged from the pictures and the change report. Passes.**

## How the request became the change

- The lawns a fresh green, every tree the blossom one, a high clear sun. Blossom first came out a dark
  purple in the views: the leaf material (`grass`) mixes in brown-green, and the painted light darkened the
  crowns' undersides. Blossom is now a plain painted pink and a tree's crowns have no painted light; the
  shaded side of a crown still reads mauve in a view against the sun, as pink in shade does, and pink
  in the model gallery.

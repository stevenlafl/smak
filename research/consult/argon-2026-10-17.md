### 1. Is the bar reachable for each kind, and where is each capped?

Under the current rubric ([rubric.md](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/rubric.md) and [scoring.mjs](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/scoring.mjs#L42-L54)), an asset passes only when **every must-have item scores `2`** (six to eight items per asset) **and** its total is at least 85%; a kind passes when at least 80% of its assets pass. That conjunctive gate means a single `1` on any must-have item fails an otherwise strong asset. With that bar in mind, the seven asset kinds divide into three groups:

#### Reachable now (one-shot on a strong maker, or 2–3 revision rounds on Haiku)
* **Sounds** and **Sprites**: Already passed 100% (4/4 each) on the standard picture/description/brief legs ([scores.md](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/scores.md#L7-L16)). In the original mod ([scores-made.md](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/original/scores-made.md#L21-L29)), failures come not from the kit's synthesis primitives ([src/spec/sound.mjs](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/src/spec/sound.mjs), [src/spec/sprite.mjs](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/src/spec/sprite.mjs)), which are expressive enough for GoldSrc, but from **one-shot maker compliance** with acoustic/visual envelopes (e.g., an alarm buzzing continuously instead of repeating pulses, or an electric arc drawn as an almond silhouette). A 2-round revision loop with spectrogram/strip feedback will reliably clear 80%+.
* **Props** (static and jointed architectural/industrial objects): Passed 5/5 on the standard legs ([scores.md](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/scores.md#L7-L11)) and 2–3 of 5 in the original mod ([scores-made.md](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/original/scores-made.md#L9-L13)). The constructive CSG/loft/lathe/tube vocabulary in [src/spec/model.mjs](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/src/spec/model.mjs) and procedural painter fit man-made props well. Where props fail (`fw_pump`, `fw_floodlight`), the cap is **maker spatial reasoning on compound assemblies** (parts floating or clipping into adjacent blocks, which trips `M3`/`M7`) and **judge variance**. Revisions with rendered sheets plus an automatic part-intersection/gap check in `build` can bring props past 80%.
* **`.wad` textures**: Passed 4/4 on single-texture legs ([scores.md](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/scores.md#L14)), but failed as a 14-texture set in the original mod (`9/12`, `T1: 1, T2: 1, T5: 1` in [fw.wad judge output](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/original/judge/made/fw.wad_the_mod_s_textures_concrete_brick_rusted_and_painted_metal_grating_tile_wood_and_water.json)). Twelve of the fourteen textures were praised; the entire `.wad` failed because `!fw_water` had low static Laplacian detail (`2.8` vs. Valve's overall 5th-percentile `10.8`, ignoring that GoldSrc's `!water` textures are smooth tiles meant to be sine-warped by the engine) and `fw_concwet` lacked a wet sheen. Capped today by **all-or-nothing set judging** and the **measured detail gate on water/glass**, not by the texture painter.

#### Reachable with revision loops + targeted kit extensions (currently capped at ~50–75%)
* **Weapons** (`v_`, `p_`, `w_` sets and melee): The domain kit in [kits/firearmkit.mjs](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/kits/firearmkit.mjs) proved that high-level parametric assembly on top of Valve's arm skeleton works: `shotgun` hit `18/18` once and `16/18` (`M4: 1, M7: 1`) on a 3-round rerun ([examples/reproduce/held/weapons/shotgun/brief/rounds.json](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/held/weapons/shotgun/brief/rounds.json)). However, `firearmkit.mjs` only covers long guns; `crowbar` (`13/18`, [examples/reproduce/held/weapons/crowbar/brief/judge.json](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/held/weapons/crowbar/brief/judge.json)) failed because the maker had to hand-coordinate a bent hexagonal bar and angled claw across `v_`, `p_`, and `w_` models without a melee kit, and its dark flat-painted iron bands were read by the judge as "black gaps." Capped by **missing domain kits for pistols, melee, and sci-fi weapons** and **2–3 point judge spread on `M4`/`M7`**.

#### Currently hard-capped below the bar (0% pass rate)
* **Maps**: One-shot maps top out around 12–16 of 18 (`fw_pumphall` reached 16/18 once and 12/18 in [examples/reproduce/original/judge/made/maps_fw_pumphall.bsp_the_pump_hall_made_from_the_mod_s_brief_with_its_models_and_textures.json](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/original/judge/made/maps_fw_pumphall.bsp_the_pump_hall_made_from_the_mod_s_brief_with_its_models_and_textures.json)). They are capped by **the kit's box-room grammar** in [src/spec/map.mjs](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/src/spec/map.mjs) (axial cuboids without chamfered corners, wall trim/pilasters, ceiling beams, or sloped terrain) **and** by **how maps are photographed for the judge** in [original.mjs](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/original.mjs#L168-L176) (more on this in Sections 3 and 4).
* **Characters and creatures**: Currently 0 of 4 on held briefs ([scores.md](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/scores.md#L18-L32)). Interestingly, in the original mod, `the_crawler` (`models/fw_crawler.mdl`) scored **19 of 20** (`M1` through `C2` all `2`, except `M2: 1`) and failed *solely* because `size` is omitted on `enemy` in [examples/reproduce/original/assets.json](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/original/assets.json#L16), passing `sizeAsked: null` into [the_crawler judge output](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/original/judge/made/models_fw_crawler.mdl_the_crawler_four-legged_pale_sewer_creature_with_idle_walk_leap_and_death.json), where the Haiku judge docked `M2` to `1` because no numerical target was provided to check the 15% tolerance against! Even so, for human characters and canonical Half-Life creatures (`barney`, `scientist`, `zombie`, `houndeye`), the cap is **fundamental to the current generator + texturing + rigid-skinning pipeline**, not prompt engineering or revision count.

### 2. Fidelity of characters and creatures: from the right two figures to Valve's Barney

Looking at `/tmp/claude-1000/-media-Data3-Packages-smak/50cffc54-6c07-4d66-acee-62220e174352/scratchpad/consult-barney-pair.png` alongside [examples/reproduce/held/characters/barney/brief/spec.json](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/held/characters/barney/brief/spec.json) and [answer.md](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/held/characters/barney/brief/answer.md), the right-hand model failed for two compounding reasons:

1. **What the maker did on that build**: The maker called `generate` (`hunyuan3d-mini` with SD-Turbo over the Gordon stance guide), saw that SD-Turbo's 4-step 512×512 image lacked the black vest and `"SECURITY"` print, **threw away the generated picture (`project` was omitted)**, painted the entire generated body a procedural blue-violet `"cloth"` (whose horizontal `fbm` bands wrap across the legs and torso), and bolted primitive boxes/superquadrics on top (`vest` `[16.5, 21.5, 15]`, `helmet` `[13, 12, 10]`, `visor` `[2, 9, 3]`), burying the head completely. The `"SECURITY"` label (`height: 3` on a low-res procedural canvas) rasterized into unreadable yellow dots (`.. .. ... .`).
2. **Why even the best generator builds in [research/generated-characters.md](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/research/generated-characters.md) still fall short**:
   * **Single-view 512×512 SD-Turbo (`sd-turbo`, 4 steps)** in [generators/worker.py](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/generators/worker.py#L221-L261) has neither the spatial resolution nor the prompt adherence to draw crisp 1998-style equipment (collar, tie, badge, holster, pouches, facial features, back stencil).
   * **Single-view Hunyuan3D-2mini** hallucinates unseen side/back depth as pillowy, inflated blobs (merging the helmet into the shoulders like a hood and rounding off planar chest/boot facets).
   * **QEM decimation to 480–650 tris + `smooth: 80`** produces isotropic triangles that neither follow anatomical articulation creases nor have GoldSrc's crisp low-poly crease angles; `27` triangles still span across the armpits/crotch ([research/generated-characters.md](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/research/generated-characters.md#L121-L135)), tearing when animated because GoldSrc has **rigid single-bone-per-vertex skinning**.
   * **Front-only projection with `carry: true`** ([src/spec/project.mjs](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/src/spec/project.mjs#L439-L471)) projects one front view onto faces with $\cos\theta > 0.2$ and fills every back/side texel by copying the front colour at the same `(right, up)` coordinate—smearing the front face onto the back of the head and the front shirt/tie onto the back of the torso.

#### Concrete route to Valve's `barney.mdl` on an AMD RX 9070 (16 GB VRAM, ROCm) + 60 GB RAM

To get from the right two figures to the left two within 700–1600 triangles and 8-bit textures, **stop treating a rigged GoldSrc human as a single welded watertight blob**. Look at how Valve's `barney.mdl` (left two figures) is actually built:
* **Crisp, faceted, per-limb sub-parts** (or clean limb cuts along bone boundaries) so that when `Bip01 UpperArm` or `Bip01 Thigh` rotates, polygons pivot cleanly without stretching long triangles across the armpit or groin.
* **High-contrast, hand-painted-style multi-view textures** (front, back, left, right) where 80% of the visual detail—the face, eyes, collar, tie, silver badge, pocket seams, belt buckles, holster strap, and white `"SECURITY"` stencil on the back of the vest—lives in the **256-colour texture**, not in micro-geometry.

Here is the pipeline that fits comfortably in 16 GB VRAM on ROCm (using sequential model offloading in [generators/worker.py](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/generators/worker.py) so only one heavy pipeline resides on GPU at a time, caching intermediate latents/images in the 60 GB system RAM):

1. **Replace 4-step single-view SD-Turbo with a multi-view orthographic turnaround sheet (Flux.1-schnell NF4/int8 or SDXL + ControlNet Depth/Pose + multi-view LoRA)**:
   * Render the target skeleton's base mesh (in `GENERATED_STANCE`) from **four orthographic views** (`front` yaw 0, `right` yaw 270, `back` yaw 180, `left` yaw 90) into a single $1024\times1024$ $2\times2$ depth + normal grid.
   * Run **SDXL-Lightning (4–8 steps, fp16, ~6.5 GB VRAM)** with `controlnet-depth-sdxl` + `controlnet-normal-sdxl` (or **Flux.1-schnell Q4/Q8** via `bitsandbytes`/GGUF, ~9–11 GB VRAM) to generate all four views simultaneously in one $1024\times1024$ turnaround image. Generating all 4 views in a single $2\times2$ grid with shared self-attention forces consistent colours, vest height, belt line, and helmet rim across front, sides, and back, while allowing explicit front/back detail (tie and badge in the front quadrant; `"SECURITY"` stencil in the back quadrant).
2. **Use Multi-View 3D Reconstruction or Anatomical Part-Templates**:
   * **For creatures and organic shapes (`houndeye`, `zombie`, `bullsquid`)**: Feed the 4-view turnaround into **Hunyuan3D-2mv** (multi-view conditioned DiT, fp16, ~8 GB VRAM after unloading the image model) instead of single-view `hunyuan3d-mini`. Multi-view conditioning eliminates the back-side blobbing and hood-helmet merge because the side and back silhouettes constrain the SDF directly.
   * **For human characters on Valve bipeds (`barney`, `scientist`, `hgrunt`, `hassassin`)**: Even better than a raw diffusion mesh is a **parametric articulated body kit (`characterkit.mjs`)** or **per-limb mesh segmentation**:
     * Before decimation, **slice the generated high-res mesh along the skeleton's joint planes** (neck, shoulders, elbows, wrists, waist, hips, knees, ankles) using the bone Voronoi / plane-cut boundaries in `GENERATED_STANCE`, cap or slightly overlap the joint sockets (just as Valve's `barney` and `hgrunt` overlap limb cylinders at the shoulder and hip), and **decimate each bone's segment independently** with locked boundary loops.
     * This guarantees **zero `span > 1` triangles (`far = 0`)**, eliminating the 27 armpit/groin tearing triangles in [research/generated-characters.md](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/research/generated-characters.md#L127) and giving crisp low-poly limb articulation (`smooth: 45` instead of `80`).
3. **4-View Texture Projection (`paintProjections`) + View-Aligned UV Charts ([src/charts.mjs](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/src/charts.mjs))**:
   * Do not pack generated character meshes into tiny `gridAtlas` per-triangle cells (which waste half the texels on 3-pixel bleed borders and quantize diagonal edges into staircase blocks). Instead, split each body segment into **4 view-aligned charts** (`front`, `back`, `left`, `right` via `charts.mjs` / [research/character-texturing.md](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/research/character-texturing.md#L64-L81)) and project all four quadrants of the $1024\times1024$ turnaround sheet via [paintProjections](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/src/spec/project.mjs#L250) (`views: [{ yaw: 0 }, { yaw: 90 }, { yaw: 180 }, { yaw: 270 }]`).
   * **Fix the bounding-box bug in `paintProjections`** ([src/spec/project.mjs](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/src/spec/project.mjs#L251-L263)): Right now, `all` computes the bounding box over *all* meshes in the model. If the maker adds a holstered pistol, helmet visor, or claws (`fingers`), the bounding box shifts and misaligns the projection across the entire body! `paintProjections` should compute `[a0, a1, h0, h1]` only over the target parts (`view.parts`) or the generated mesh itself.
   * **Hybrid overlay pass**: Allow `paint` commands (`text`, `stripe`, `rect`, `noise`) to layer **on top of** `project` output (rather than forcing a choice between procedural material and projected image), and render `text` at $2\times$ supersampled resolution before palette quantization so `"SECURITY"` is sharp at 3–4 units tall.

### 3. Maps: what would most raise a map made from a brief toward Valve's own?

Looking at the statistical gap in [research/map-geometry.md](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/research/map-geometry.md) (Valve campaign maps average 517 world brushes with a 42% non-axial vertex share, plus 60 `func_wall` detail entities, vs. our heightplan/spec maps with 0% non-axial vertices and box rooms) and the judge reports for `fw_yard`, `fw_pumphall`, and `fw_control` in [examples/reproduce/original/judge/made/](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/original/judge/made/), four changes would raise map scores and true visual fidelity the most:

1. **Fix how maps are photographed for the judge and maker (`views.png` and `plan.png`)**:
   * In `fw_pumphall` ([judge output](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/original/judge/made/maps_fw_pumphall.bsp_the_pump_hall_made_from_the_mod_s_brief_with_its_models_and_textures.json)), the maker actually built the flooded lower floor, the three pumps, the upper catwalks, the bridge, the ladder, and the stairs—and the plan showed them—yet the map scored `12/18` (`P3: 1, P5: 1, P7: 1, P9: 1`) because `maps.viewsOf(bsp, { count: 8 })` ([original.mjs](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/original.mjs#L173)) placed cameras blindly where the lower halves of three views clipped into dark walls and **not a single view looked at the catwalks, bridge, or water!**
   * Likewise, `maps.planOf(bsp, { heights: true, legend: true })` ([original.mjs](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/original.mjs#L172)) drew floor elevations as colour without numerical height labels in `fw_pumphall` and `fw_yard`, causing the judge to dock `P3: 1` ("The plan shows floor heights only as colour, with no numbers").
   * **Fix**: Always print numeric `z=` floor labels in each room/landing on `plan.png` and add a **multi-storey / section strip** (`map-section` or `map-storeys`) plus **feature-targeted cameras** in `viewsOf` (automatically aiming cameras at each vertical transition, water volume, prop cluster, and light fixture, rejecting views with >25% black or <64 units mean depth).
2. **Add automatic architectural framing ("room dressing") to [src/spec/map.mjs](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/src/spec/map.mjs)**:
   * When an LLM writes `{ "room": "hall", "box": [...] }`, it builds six bare slabs. Valve never leaves a 280-tall room as four flat 280-tall walls: Valve cuts walls horizontally into a **dado/baseboard band** (32–64 high), a **main wall band**, and an **upper frieze/cornice**, and places **wall pilasters/ribs** every 192–256 units, **ceiling cross-beams**, **45° corner chamfers** (`cut`), and **door/opening frames** around every portal.
   * Adding declarative room-framing options to [src/spec/map.mjs](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/src/spec/map.mjs)—e.g., `trim: { dado: { height: 48, texture: "..." }, ribs: { step: 192, size: [16, 16], texture: "..." }, ceilingBeams: { step: 192, size: [24, 32] }, chamfer: 32, doorframe: { width: 8, depth: 16 } }`—turns a 6-brush box into a 35-brush Half-Life industrial hall with angled corners and structural rhythm, without asking the LLM to compute 30 brush coordinates by hand.
3. **Texlight fixtures + ambient/bounce lighting presets instead of bare floating point lights**:
   * Valve lights Half-Life maps primarily through recessed light-fixture brushes (`+0~light...` with `lights.rad` surface emission) paired with a slightly offset `light` / `light_spot` and a subtle cool/warm ambient floor, whereas makers drop bare point `light` entities in mid-air (`P6` failures in `fw_yard` and `fw_pumphall`).
   * Make every `light` in a map spec optionally emit a small physical fixture brush on the nearest ceiling/wall (using the `.wad`'s light texture) plus a two-tier light pair (a local warm/sodium pool + a weak wide fill so lower floors in multi-storey rooms don't bake to solid black).
4. **Enforce wall-band texture alignment and prevent vertical stretching**:
   * In `fw_yard` and `fw_pumphall`, judges flagged concrete and brick textures reading as "stretched plank-like bands" or repeating monotonously over 280-unit walls. Automatically splitting tall walls at `z + 64` and `z + 192` with a trim strip and world-aligned UVs stops 128×128 textures from tiling 3× vertically down a bare slab.

### 4. Is the rubric's design sound, and what should change to measure fidelity over noise?

The rubric's core structure ([examples/reproduce/rubric.md](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/rubric.md))—combining **controls** (Valve's own assets must pass), **decoys** (perturbed assets must fail), **objective measured gates** (`M1`, `M2`, `M6`, `C1`, `P1`, `P4`, `P8`, `T2`, `S1`, `A1`–`A4`), and **blinded line-ups**—is fundamentally much stronger than typical LLM-as-judge benchmarks. However, five specific flaws currently inject heavy noise and false negatives into the scores:

1. **The character/creature line-up pool mixes humans and creatures ([examples/reproduce/brief.mjs](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/brief.mjs#L78-L96))**:
   * `ownOfKind("character")` concatenates `[...PEOPLE, ...CREATURES]` into a single pool and picks the 3 models with the closest bounding-box diagonal (`lineupOf`).
   * Because `houndeye` has diagonal `73.2`, its "peers" in the line-up are three human bipeds: `gman.mdl` (`72.7`), `scientist.mdl` (`74.0`), and `hassassin.mdl` (`75.3`) ([houndeye judge.json](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/held/characters/houndeye/brief/judge.json#L25-L30)). Conversely, `scientist` is lined up alongside `houndeye`! Even Valve's own `houndeye` stands out as the only 3-legged dog in a line-up of three standing men, and any generated quadruped in a line-up of three humans is caught 4/4 times (`failedByMeasure: ["M6"]`, automatic `0/18`).
   * **Fix**: Split `ownOfKind` into `"human"` (`PEOPLE`) and `"creature"` (`CREATURES`) so bipeds are lined up only against bipeds and creatures only against creatures.
2. **A harness bug when `size` is omitted fails `M2` automatically ([examples/reproduce/brief.mjs](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/brief.mjs#L218) vs. [examples/reproduce/original/assets.json](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/original/assets.json#L16))**:
   * When an asset has no numerical `size` in `assets.json` (like `the_crawler`, which says *"the size of a headcrab"*), `sizeAsked` is passed to the judge as `null`. Although `brief.mjs` sets `caps.M2 = 2`, the Haiku judge still scores `M2` itself and gives `M2: 1` because *"sizeAsked is null, so there is no number to hold the measure to within 15%"* ([the_crawler judge output](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/original/judge/made/models_fw_crawler.mdl_the_crawler_four-legged_pale_sewer_creature_with_idle_walk_leap_and_death.json)). Because `M2` is a must-have item, `the_crawler` failed despite scoring **19/20**!
   * **Fix**: Either populate explicit `size` targets for every asset in `assets.json` (e.g., `[24, 24, 14]` for `the_crawler`), or make `M2` purely deterministic in code (`score.M2 = caps.M2`) instead of asking the LLM to do arithmetic on bounding boxes.
3. **Only models get 2 judge readings; maps, `.wad`s, sprites, sounds, and the mod get only 1 ([examples/reproduce/brief.mjs](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/brief.mjs#L163), [examples/reproduce/judge.mjs](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/judge.mjs#L412))**:
   * Under a strict conjunctive gate where **all 6 to 8 must-have items must equal `2`**, if a single Haiku call has even a 12% chance per subjective item of giving a `1` instead of a `2` on a borderline-good asset, an asset with 6 subjective must-have items passes only $(1 - 0.12)^6 \approx 46\%$ of the time—matching the observed 2–3 point spread.
   * Worse, `combine(..., "better")` (`max` over 2 runs in [brief.mjs](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/brief.mjs#L163)) is only run for `model` in `brief.mjs` and is **not used at all in `original.mjs`** (except on `detect` line-ups), and `max` of 2 runs biases upward on decoys while leaving single-run kinds completely exposed to 1-point noise.
   * **Fix**: Use **3 independent judge calls (`temperature: 0.2` or `0.3`) combined by per-item median (majority vote)** across *all* asset kinds, and make purely numerical items (`M1`, `M2`, `C1`, `P1`, `P4`, `P8`, `S1`, `A1`–`A4`) deterministic in [scoring.mjs](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/scoring.mjs) so the LLM only scores visual/semantic items.
4. **Special-case water/scrolling textures in `T2` ([examples/reproduce/brief.mjs](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/brief.mjs#L337-L343)) and score `.wad` textures per-texture**:
   * Exemption or a lower Laplacian detail floor (`>= 2.0` instead of `10.8`) is needed for `!water` textures in `T2`, because GoldSrc warps `!` textures dynamically at runtime and smooth water tiles are penalized as "flat". Also, score a `.wad` by the fraction of its textures that pass (e.g., $\ge 85\%$) rather than failing a 14-texture `.wad` when 12 of 14 are good.

*Where I am unsure*: I have not seen a multi-run variance matrix of the **controls** (`examples/reproduce/control-cache.json`) across 5+ fresh Haiku seeds without caching. Running the controls 5 times uncached would tell us immediately which rubric items (`M4`, `M7`, `P6`, `P7`) have the highest intrinsic Haiku variance on Valve's own assets.

### 5. The three highest-leverage things to do next, in order

#### 1. Fix the evaluation and camera harness (`brief.mjs`, `original.mjs`, `maps.viewsOf`, `maps.planOf`)
* **What to do**:
  1. Separate `PEOPLE` and `CREATURES` in `ownOfKind()` ([examples/reproduce/brief.mjs](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/brief.mjs#L78)).
  2. Make numerical items (`M2`, `P8`, `S1`, `A1`–`A3`) deterministic in code and add an explicit `size` to `the_crawler` in [examples/reproduce/original/assets.json](file:///media/Data3/Packages/smak/examples/reproduce/original/assets.json#L16).
  3. Exempt `!` water textures from the `10.8` Laplacian floor in `T2`.
  4. Fix `maps.planOf` to always print numeric floor heights (`z=0`, `z=128`) and `maps.viewsOf` to aim cameras at each storey, water body, and entity cluster (plus include a `map-section` strip for the judge).
